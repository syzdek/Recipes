Bacon Blue Cheese Stuffed Mushtooms
-----------------------------------

Ingredients:
       8 oz   bacon
      24 ---  mushrooms, large button
       4 ---  garlic cloves, minced
       5 oz   blue cheese, room temperature
       8 oz   cream cheese, room temperature
       2 tbl  fresh chives, minced

Directions:
   1. Chop bacon into small pieces and brown in heavy pan. Transfer bacon to
      plate with paper towel. Drain all but 2 tbl of grease from pan.
   2. Remove dirt from mushrooms. Remove bottom of stems and discard.  Remove
      remaining stems and chop.
   3. Cook chopped stems in bacon grease for 3-5 minutes. Add minced garlic and
      continue cooking for another 2 minutes.
   4. Combine blue cheese, cream cheese, chives, bacon, and cooked stems.
   5. Fill each mushroom cap with cheese filling.
   6. Bake at 375 F for 20 minutes. Serve warm.
